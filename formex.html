About Our college

J.B. Institute of Engineering & Technology (JBIET, often referred to as JBIT) is a prominent private engineering college in Hyderabad, Telangana. Established in 1998, it is affiliated with JNTUH, accredited 'A' grade by NAAC, and offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
