HAMPI

Hampi, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is home to spectacular ancient monuments. The region is divided into the Sacred Centre (riverfront) and the Royal Centre (inland). Major temples, such as the Virupaksha and Vijaya Vittala, host daily pujas, architectural viewings, and sunset or sunrise treks.

Vijayanagara Research Project(VRP)

Over more than two decades, during consecutive field seasons, the two scholars co-directed teams of volunteer students of architecture and archaeology from India and abroad. Together, they meticulously mapped and measured cultural features spread across nearly 25 square kilometres, creating an unparalleled record of the site.
